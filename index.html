Crypto rewards

How to check rewards safely

Promotions change frequently. A reward may be unavailable in Ethiopia or limited to selected accounts.

Binance reward-check checklist

Use the official Binance app or website. Never trust a message just because it uses the Binance logo.

  1. Open Binance directly from the app you installed.
  2. Check Rewards Hub and any available campaign pages.
  3. Read country eligibility, task requirements and expiry dates.
  4. Confirm whether the reward is a token, fee rebate or trading voucher.
  5. Never deposit or trade just to chase a small reward.
Visit Binance Support ↗
Important: This website is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Binance. Campaign availability and terms can change. Check official terms before taking part.

Reward reality check

  • “Free” rewards may require eligible tasks or identity checks.
  • Some vouchers cannot be withdrawn as cash or USDT.
  • Red Packet codes can expire or run out.
  • Never pay an admin to release a gift.
  • Check local laws and platform availability in Ethiopia.
Protect yourself

Five rules to avoid earning scams

Pause and verify before sending money, sharing information or connecting a wallet.

🚫

No unlock fees

Do not pay a fee, tax or deposit to claim a supposed giveaway prize.

🔑

Keep secrets private

Never share your password, one-time code, private key or recovery phrase.

🔎

Verify the source

Open official apps and type official website addresses yourself. Beware of lookalike links.

💸

Reject guaranteed profit

Promises of fixed daily returns, doubled crypto or risk-free trading are major warning signs.

🧾

Read the conditions

Check fees, withdrawal rules, country restrictions and task requirements first.

🛑

Stop if pressured

Do not rush because a stranger says an offer expires in minutes. Verify independently.

Common questions

Before you begin

Can this website make money immediately?

No. A website does not earn automatically. You need useful original content, visitors, a suitable monetization method and compliance with each platform's rules. Income is never guaranteed.

Can people in Ethiopia use every crypto rewards offer?

No. Each service and campaign has its own country, identity-verification and account eligibility rules. Check current official terms for Ethiopia before participating.

Do I need to pay to claim a Red Packet?

You should not pay a stranger or channel admin to unlock a gift. Be alert for fake codes, impersonation and phishing websites.

Is this website financial advice?

No. It is general educational information, not investment, legal or tax advice. Crypto assets can lose value.
